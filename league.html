Official Team Requirements

To participate in the Pro Tactical League, teams must meet the following criteria before the season begins:

Roster Size

A minimum of 4 active players and up to 2 substitutes must be registered per team.

Age Limit

All registered competitive players must be at least 16 years of age by the start of the season.

Uniforms

Teams must have matching colored dark attire or official team jerseys for all matches.
