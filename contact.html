Common Intel (FAQ)

What is the minimum age to play?
Operatives must be at least 7 years old to handle the tactical gear safely. Guardians must sign waivers for all minors.
Do I need to book in advance?
Advanced booking is highly recommended, especially for weekends and holiday cycles. Walk-ins are subject to arena availability.
How long does each session last?
Standard missions are 20 minutes long, including a 5-minute tactical briefing and gear-up sequence.
What is the mandatory dress code?
Comfortable activewear and closed-toe shoes are mandatory. No sandals or high heels are permitted within the combat grid.
